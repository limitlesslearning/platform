# Using the Klados logo

Short version: **copy it, use it, share it. Just don't use it to mislead people.**

## You're welcome to

- Use the logo in posts, talks, slides, videos, blog articles and course material about Klados.
- Put it on your fork, your study group, your meetup page or your community project.
- Print stickers, T-shirts and posters.
- Remix it, draw your own versions, and make derivatives.
- Download any file in this kit. No need to ask.

## The one ask

Please don't use the logo in a way that makes people believe something that isn't true. For example:

- Fake badges, certificates or "verified by Klados" seals.
- Courses, apps or services that pretend to be official Klados when they aren't.
- Scams, phishing, or anything that asks learners for money or data in the project's name.

If your project is related but not run by the Klados project, a simple line like "community project, not affiliated with Klados" keeps things clear.

## Getting it right

- Use the files in `svg/` and `png/` instead of redrawing them.
- On a dark background, use the `inverse` or `white` files.
- Leave some empty space around it, and don't fill in the open rings. They mean "the next step is open."
- The full design rules are in `BRAND.md`.

## Seen misuse?

If you see the logo used to mislead learners, please open an issue in the Klados repository with a link.
